How To Play

Drop Phase: Take turns placing your 3 pieces. Get 3 in a row to win immediately.

Move Phase: After all pieces are placed, move one piece per turn to an adjacent point. First to align 3 wins.

Stalemate: If the same position repeats twice, it's a draw.

House Rules: Move to any empty spot (not just adjacent).
